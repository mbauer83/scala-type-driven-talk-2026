`story-strip`. P5 is the original diagnosis and it is worth restating, because
this pattern recurs on every payoff slide in the deck: two parallel scoreboards
doing the same job badly; checklist-ese nobody reads at projection distance;
a `CLOSES` column that gives the answer away before the suspense exists;
strikethrough that dies on a projector; and about fifteen seconds each time,
five times over.

line of code that is now unwriteable. The collective view happens **once**, at
the end, on the dark *Unrepresentable* slide, which already works and is the
emotional peak.

Stage 4 lands.
